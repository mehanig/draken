# 0.8.1 reuse qualification

This records local candidate qualification, not publication or deployment.

## Contract

- Previously included, unreviewed entries remain eligible. Inclusion, completed processing and explicit review are separate facts.
- Reuse requires complete compatible primary/background inputs, model/recipe identity and verified originals. Citations and source counts do not establish coverage.
- Keep completed tails, interleaved leaves, compatible parent plans and successful cancelled work. Preserve historical node provenance across same-snapshot retries; current-run markers control progress display.
- Initial E/C retains its captured projection while current source consent and physical-original checks remain mandatory. Added dependencies do not acquire initial-scope authority through persistence or reopening.
- Retain distinct admitted selected-pointer representations from **all** leaf paths, including primary inputs and restored pending work. A matching model-facing key cannot erase known receipt provenance.
- Canonical references prefer E, then first-known C. Verify every additional representation without overwriting those references.
- Check full dependencies before spending, at cache/native dispatch boundaries, after asynchronous inference and before persistence. Preserve cleanup ownership and existing transcripts on refusal.

Embedding rollover tests use real temporary SQLite databases and original-file fixtures with synthetic embedding results. They check unchanged vector IDs/hashes/bytes and exact new inference, including durable-commit cancellation/reopen. They do not simulate an operating-system worker kill or qualify real embedding weights.

A guarded private-copy plan audit covered 24,339 entries: 23,527 reused cached processing, 812 remained uncovered, and **none of the 22,849 previously included entries required new leaf processing**. Cached and uncovered coverage did not overlap. Audit output contained aggregate counts only; Draken provider calls were forbidden and remained zero. This is a sample, not a guarantee that changed originals or model/recipe changes require no work. Warm plans performed no historical replay or pending-packing passes.

## Independent review

Fresh read-only review repeatedly found gaps beyond green suites: retry provenance deletion, final-call gates, persisted dependency promotion, live admission, reduced legacy views, same-key distinct pointers, primary-pointer retention, canonical C replacement and restored pending inputs. Each was reproduced and corrected rather than waived.

The final fresh independent review reported **no worthwhile findings remaining within its requested scope**. It independently exercised 168 selected regressions/prior reproductions and 15 newly written challenges against matching current-source checksums. Challenges included revocation during inference/live operation, physical tampering, receipt/alias preservation, pending and direct leaf paths, and stable canonical representatives. An earlier separate voice review also independently exercised durable scope and asynchronous native boundaries.

Implementation-worker reports were not treated as independent approval. The integrated parent qualification above was rerun after the final source changes.

## Limits

All application model/native boundaries in qualification were synthetic. Real-provider answer quality, real embedding weights and physical-device audio acceptance remain unclaimed. Already-transmitted provider data/audio cannot be recalled; gates prevent subsequent application dispatch once revocation is detected. Original verification and metadata work remain necessary even when inference is reused, so warm cache does not imply instantaneous preparation.
